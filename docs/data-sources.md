## Before sharing this app

Read on 2026-10-08. Each point below is a reason this app is a personal tool today.

- **Cesium ion Community plan is personal and non-commercial.** The pricing page heads it "Personal and non-commercial use". Its FAQ says a paid account is needed "If your company currently makes more than $50K in annual gross revenues or has raised funds in excess of $50K, or / If you are working on a government project, or / If you are working on funded educational research, or / If you exceed the usage limits of the free community account". Limits: 15 GB streaming and 1,000 global-imagery sessions a month (https://cesium.com/platform/cesium-ion/pricing/). The ion token is baked into local builds, so restrict it to your URLs in the ion dashboard before any deployment.
- **Mapillary uploads are public, under CC BY-SA.** Terms section 4.2: uploading grants Mapillary "a nonexclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable, worldwide, fully paid, transferable and sublicensable license ... for a period of ninety-nine (99) years", and "your User Content and any associated information (such as your username, profile photo, and bio) may be visible to others". Section 3.2: other users get it under "the Creative Commons Share Alike (CC BY-SA) license". Never upload photos of private places you would not publish. Section 5 bans use "in connection with real-time navigation ... or with any systems or functions for automatic or autonomous control of vehicle or device behavior" (https://www.mapillary.com/terms, effective 15 Feb 2024).
- **ArduPilot is GPL-3.0.** Shipping its code or binaries inside a distributed product brings GPL obligations. Running it as a separate program that talks MAVLink is how this app uses it; take advice before any commercial distribution (plan p.19, docs/repos.md).
- **Imagery and data licences:** Sentinel-2 cloudless is non-commercial only (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). OpenStreetMap data is ODbL, and Mapillary, KartaView and Panoramax images are CC BY-SA: any shared copy keeps their credits.
