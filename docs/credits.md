# Credits and licences of bundled assets

## Drone model: `web/public/models/drone.gltf`
- **What:** a simple "+" quadcopter, about 0.6 m across. It has a dark body, grey arms, light rotor discs, a red nose at the front and a green right-hand rotor (like an aircraft's starboard light).
- **Made by:** this project, generated by `node scripts/make-drone-model.mjs` (own geometry, no third-party model or texture). Generated 2026-10-09.
- **Licence:** our own work, free to reuse anywhere with the project. glTF 2.0, embedded buffer, about 11 KB.
- **Why not a downloaded model:** the overnight run of 2026-10-09 could download only the authorised SITL files. Replace this model with a nicer free glTF (CC0 or CC-BY) when you want, and record its licence here.
- **Axes, measured on screen 2026-10-09 (`web/e2e/drone.mjs`):**
  - The nose is on glTF +Z, which Cesium treats as the forward axis of a glTF 2.0 model. That axis points east at Cesium heading 0, so the app adds -90 to the compass heading (`MODEL_HEADING_OFFSET_DEG`).
  - Pitch +30 raises the nose. Roll +30 lowers the right side, the same signs as ArduPilot.
  - A first version had the nose on +X. Cesium's pitch then turned it about its nose and its roll about its wings; seen from above, the nose span shrank from 26 to 18.5 px under Cesium roll.
